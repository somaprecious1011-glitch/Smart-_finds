03 / SHOP SMARTER

Five checks before you buy

  1. 01
    Confirm your needs.

    Write down the problem you want the product to solve.

  2. 02
    Compare the full cost.

    Include delivery, subscriptions, accessories and possible fees.

  3. 03
    Read a range of reviews.

    Look for specific details and repeated issues, not just star ratings.

  4. 04
    Check returns and warranty.

    Understand the seller's terms before paying.

  5. 05
    Use trustworthy links.

    Check the seller and website address. Never share passwords or one-time codes.
